\item \textbf{1.5B$\to$0.5B.} The 0.5B model struggles at this length even reading natively: A$'$ \res{q.15b-05b.A2.longneedle}, B \res{q.15b-05b.B.longneedle}. With the transferred cache it is worse still: C \res{q.15b-05b.C.longneedle}, D \res{q.15b-05b.D.longneedle}.

The time the transfer saves is real, about 3\,s for the 3B pair.
What it saves is the time needed to get the answer right.
