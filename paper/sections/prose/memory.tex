\paragraph{The band exists where the arithmetic says, for both pairs.}
\Cref{fig:survival,tab:memory} report 3 runs per cell, each in its own process under the watchdog.

\emph{1.5B$\to$0.5B.} The big model decodes at $S=\res{m.15b-05b.S}$\,MiB.
A co-resident switch needs $\approx$\res{m.15b-05b.need}\,MiB of headroom: \res{m.15b-05b.dstw}\,MiB of receiver weights, the \res{m.15b-05b.mapw}\,MiB mapper and a margin.

\emph{3B$\to$1.5B.} $S=\res{m.3b-15b.S}$\,MiB, and the need is $\approx$\res{m.3b-15b.need}\,MiB (\res{m.3b-15b.dstw}\,MiB of weights and a \res{m.3b-15b.mapw}\,MiB mapper).

Doing nothing (\emph{big, ignores pressure}) is killed in every cell of both pairs, so the emulator reproduces the kill.
The hand-off shape (C, and D in the colocated schedule) is killed in \texttt{load\_dst} or \texttt{transplant} in every cell whose headroom is below its need.
Examples are \res{m.15b-05b.h345.C.surv} at $h=345$ and \res{m.3b-15b.h1112.C.surv} at $h=1112$.
In every cell above the need it survives: \res{m.15b-05b.h569.C.surv} at $h=569$ and \res{m.3b-15b.h1336.C.surv} at $h=1336$.
Every release-first schedule survives every cell of both pairs: A, A$'$, D and C$+$ordered.
At $h=1112$ for 3B$\to$1.5B, D's kernel lifetime-maximum footprint is \res{m.3b-15b.h1112.D.peak}\,MiB under a \res{m.3b-15b.h1112.cap}\,MiB cap.
\Cref{fig:timeline} shows the footprint through one switch.

\paragraph{The naive releases fail only under MLX's default allocator.}
With MLX's default buffer cache, D with del-only is killed in every cell of both pairs, including $h=2353$ for 3B$\to$1.5B (\res{m.3b-15b.h2353.Ddelonly.surv}).
D with clear-no-wait is killed in every 3B$\to$1.5B cell and is erratic for 1.5B$\to$0.5B: \res{m.15b-05b.h128.Dclearnowait.surv}, \res{m.15b-05b.h205.Dclearnowait.surv}, \res{m.15b-05b.h345.Dclearnowait.surv}, \res{m.15b-05b.h569.Dclearnowait.surv} and \res{m.15b-05b.h818.Dclearnowait.surv} across the grid.
This matches \cref{app:mlx}: \texttt{clear\_cache} sometimes frees and sometimes does not.
With the 20\,MiB cache limit that mlx-swift's examples set on iOS, both variants survive every cell of both pairs.
For example, del-only is \res{m.c20.3b-15b.h1112.Ddelonly.surv} at $h=1112$, while C is still \res{m.c20.3b-15b.h1112.C.surv}.

\paragraph{Time to resume: re-prefill wins at the prompt lengths of experiment Q, the transfer wins at $\sim$2\,K tokens.}
With the short prompts (\res{q.15b-05b.ctx.open} tokens of context at the switch), A$'$ resumes in \res{m.15b-05b.h345.A2.resume}\,s and D in \res{m.15b-05b.h345.D.resume}\,s for 1.5B$\to$0.5B at $h=345$.
For 3B$\to$1.5B at $h=1112$ the figures are \res{m.3b-15b.h1112.A2.resume}\,s and \res{m.3b-15b.h1112.D.resume}\,s.
Releasing the sender costs every condition about \res{m.3b-15b.h1112.D.ph.releasesrc}\,s.
D additionally pays \res{m.3b-15b.h1112.D.ph.map}\,s in its \texttt{map} phase, most of it the second release (mapper and sender KV) and not the mapping itself.
A budget-aware planner could skip that release whenever the headroom allows; we did not tune D on these measurements.
With ${\sim}$2\,100-token prompts, the order reverses.
For 1.5B$\to$0.5B (cap re-measured on a long prompt, $h=345$), A$'$ takes \res{m.long.15b-05b.h345.A2.resume}\,s and D \res{m.long.15b-05b.h345.D.resume}\,s.
For 3B$\to$1.5B ($h=1112$), A$'$ takes \res{m.long.3b-15b.h1112.A2.resume}\,s and D \res{m.long.3b-15b.h1112.D.resume}\,s.
C is killed in both (\res{m.long.15b-05b.h345.C.surv}, \res{m.long.3b-15b.h1112.C.surv}).
With models resident, mapping compute alone is \res{r.3b-15b.D.P4096}\,s against \res{r.3b-15b.A2.P4096}\,s for re-prefill at 4\,096 tokens (\cref{fig:resumecost}).

\paragraph{Energy.}
CPU energy for the switch (per process) is higher for the transfer in every setting.
At short context it is \res{m.3b-15b.h1112.A2.ecpu}\,J for A$'$ against \res{m.3b-15b.h1112.D.ecpu}\,J for D (3B$\to$1.5B).
At long context it is \res{m.long.15b-05b.h345.A2.ecpu}\,J against \res{m.long.15b-05b.h345.D.ecpu}\,J (1.5B$\to$0.5B).
Re-prefill's cost, however, is mostly GPU work, and GPU energy could not be attributed: other processes drew $\sim$\res{m.3b-15b.h1112.D.gpubase}\,W of GPU baseline during these runs.
We therefore make no energy claim in either direction.
